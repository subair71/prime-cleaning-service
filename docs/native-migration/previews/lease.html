A little care goes a long way

We clean to the standard most Melbourne real estate agents check at the final inspection, room by room. We’re a small team, so the people who quote you are the people who turn up, and we take it personally when something gets missed.

What’s included

Kitchen

Benchtops, splashback, cupboards and drawers inside and out, sink and taps, rangehood filters wiped, stovetop, light switches.

Bathrooms and laundry

Shower, screen and tiles, bath, toilet, vanity and mirror, exhaust fan cover, laundry tub, taps and fittings.

Bedrooms and living areas

Wardrobes inside and out, skirting boards, doors and door frames, light switches and power points, and window sills and tracks. Window-cleaning inclusions will be confirmed in your quote.

Floors

Vacuumed and mopped throughout.

Throughout

Cobweb removal. Any wall-cleaning work will be confirmed in your quote.

What’s not included

  • High ladder work and outside windows above ground level
  • Heavy mould removal, grout restoration or biohazard cleaning
  • Rubbish removal or pest control
  • Furnished homes: we’re happy to help, but we’ll give you a custom quote

Popular add-ons

Oven clean · Fridge clean · Carpet steam cleaning · Balcony or patio · Extra wall spot cleaning

Your agent or landlord decides the bond outcome. We confirm the cleaning scope with you before work begins. Contact us promptly if your agent raises a concern about the agreed scope.

Frequently asked questions

A few helpful details before you enquire.

How long does an end of lease clean take?

Usually a few hours for an apartment and most of a day for a larger house. We’ll give you a time estimate when we confirm.

Should the home be empty?

Yes, our prices are for empty homes. If furniture is still there, ask us for a custom quote.

Do you clean the oven?

Yes, as an add-on. It’s the one appliance agents never forget to check.

Can you do carpets on the same day?

Yes. Add carpet steam cleaning in the estimator and we’ll do both in one visit.

Do I need to be there?

No. Just arrange access for the day (we don’t keep keys or record codes), and make sure water and power are still on.

Hand back the keys with confidence.

We aim to reply within 2 hours during the day, and always within 24 hours.
