Frequently asked questions

A few helpful details before you enquire.

How long does an end of lease clean take?

Usually a few hours for an apartment and most of a day for a larger house. We'll give you a time estimate when we confirm.

Should the home be empty?

Yes, our prices are for empty homes. If furniture is still there, ask us for a custom quote.

Do you clean the oven?

Yes, as an add-on. It's the one appliance agents never forget to check.

Can you do carpets on the same day?

Yes. Add carpet steam cleaning in the estimator and we'll do both in one visit.

Do I need to be there?

No. Just arrange access for the day (we don't keep keys or record codes), and make sure water and power are still on.
