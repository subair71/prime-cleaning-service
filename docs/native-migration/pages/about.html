Our story

Prime Domestic and Cleaning Services is a family business based in Malvern East. It's led by Derick, with his wife and a couple of trusted helpers, and we look after homes across Melbourne's south-east.

We started because we kept hearing the same thing from friends and neighbours: cleaners who don't turn up, prices that change on the day, and a different stranger every visit. We thought: we can do better than that.

So we keep things simple. We quote clearly, we turn up when we say we will, and we clean your home the way we'd want ours cleaned. If something isn't right, we come back and fix it.
