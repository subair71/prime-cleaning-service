Frequently asked questions

A few helpful details before you enquire.

Do I need to be home?

Not at all. Many clients arrange access for the day, like a key left in an agreed spot or a neighbour letting us in. We don't keep keys or record codes.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. We bring all equipment and products, low-tox where possible. We only need water and power on site.

Is the online price final?

It's an estimate based on what you tell us. We confirm the final price before we start, so there are no surprises.

What if I'm not happy?

Please contact us promptly with any concern about the agreed checklist so we can review it with you.

I have a dog. Is that okay?

Absolutely. We love pets, we just ask that they're secured while we work, mainly so they don't 'help' with the mop.
