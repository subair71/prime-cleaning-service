How we clean

We use hot-water extraction (often called steam cleaning). Hot water and a cleaning solution are worked deep into the fibres, then extracted along with the dirt, dust and grime that vacuuming leaves behind. It's the method most agents expect at the end of a lease, and it's great for homes with kids and pets.

Good to know

Give it time to dry

Drying time is usually a few hours, depending on weather and airflow.

An honest view on stains

Most everyday stains lift well, but some old or set-in stains (like red wine left over a weekend or permanent dye) may not fully come out. We'll always tell you honestly before we start.

Before we arrive

Please clear small items and fragile things off the floor before we arrive. We can move light furniture.

Frequently asked questions

A few helpful details before you enquire.

How long until I can walk on it?

Usually a few hours. Open windows and run fans to speed it up.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We use family-friendly, low-tox products where possible. Keep pets off until it's dry, mainly to save the paw prints.

Can you remove all stains?

Most, not all. We'll look at stubborn stains first and give you an honest view.

Can I add carpet to an end of lease clean?

Yes, choose it as an add-on and we'll do both in the same visit.
