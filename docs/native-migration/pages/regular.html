Frequently asked questions

A few helpful details before you enquire.

Will I get the same cleaner each time?

Yes. Regular clients get the same faces from our small team wherever possible.

How does the 15% discount work?

Book weekly or fortnightly and 15% comes off the cleaning price every visit. (Add-ons and travel stay at normal price.)

Can I skip a week or change my day?

Please contact us as soon as possible to discuss a change to your clean.

Do I need to provide anything?

Just water and power. We bring all equipment and products, low-tox where possible.

Is my first visit a deep clean?

If your home hasn't had a professional clean in a while, we recommend starting with a deep clean, then switching to regular.
